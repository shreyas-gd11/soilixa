About Soilixa

Helping Farmers Make Better Decisions

Soilixa is an early-stage agriculture startup focused on helping farmers improve yield through practical soil-based decisions. We combine soil data with expert guidance so farmers can choose the right crop strategy at the right time.

Our mission is to support sustainable and efficient farming with easy-to-understand recommendations that work in real field conditions.
